EchoLike v

Where your data lives

Everything EchoLike records (likes, bookmarks, written and voice notes) is stored in this browser's own extension storage. With sync off, the extension makes no network requests of its own. You can export a copy at any time, and delete it by removing the extension.

Sync (optional)

If you turn sync on, each event is encrypted in this browser (AES-256-GCM) with a key only this browser and your paired inbox hold, then relayed through ntfy.sh to the EchoLike inbox you pair with. The relay only sees unreadable text.

Inbox connection

Direct posts each event to your own inbox (instant confirmation, no codes). Legacy uses the ntfy + Telegram-bot pipe. Both sends through both (deduped server-side).







Backup
The export contains your items only, never your sync key.
