# EchoLike 2.8.0 — store listing (EN)

## Short description (132 chars max)
Save your X likes and bookmarks, with optional voice or written notes, in your own browser. Sync is off by default.

## Detailed description
EchoLike turns what you like or save on X into things you can use: re-open the post later, add a voice or written note, and export everything as JSON.

Local-first: everything is stored in your own browser. Nothing leaves your machine unless you turn on sync.

Two sync modes, your choice:
- Direct (recommended): posts each event straight to your own inbox over HTTPS. Confirmation arrives in the same response — no codes, no waiting.
- Legacy: end-to-end encrypted relay for use with a paired bot inbox.

No account, no tracking.

How it works:
1. Like or bookmark a post on X, as you already do. EchoLike picks it up.
2. Open EchoLike and see your saved posts, with notes.
3. Optional: turn on Direct sync with your inbox URL + device token.

Privacy: with sync off, the extension makes no network requests of its own. With Direct sync on, items are sent over HTTPS only to the inbox URL you configure. Your export contains your items only, never your token.
